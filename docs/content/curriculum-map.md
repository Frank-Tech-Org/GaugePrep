# French curriculum map, A1 to C1

PRD CUR-1: 5 levels, 10 units per level, 4 lessons per unit (200 lessons). Each unit is built around one communicative goal; lessons 1 to 3 build the language, lesson 4 practises it in TCF task format. Each unit ends with a unit test and each level with a level test (CUR-4).

Unit goals are our own wording based on the CEFR and spec Section 2. Grammar and vocabulary focus follow the level controls of `authoring-guide.md` 1.1.

## A1

| Unit | Communicative goal | Main language focus | TCF link |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Greet, introduce yourself and spell your name | être, s'appeler, avoir; alphabet; numbers 0 to 20 | EO1, L2 |
| 2 | Talk about your family and where you live | possessives; articles; numbers to 100 | EO1, R1 |
| 3 | Describe your day and say the time | present of -er verbs; days, hours; reflexive verbs | L2, EO1 |
| 4 | Buy food and everyday items, ask prices | partitive articles; quantities; combien | L1, R2 |
| 5 | Find your way and use transport | aller, prendre; prepositions of place; imperative of direction | L1, R1 |
| 6 | Talk about likes, dislikes and free time | aimer, préférer, faire; negation ne ... pas | EO1, L2 |
| 7 | Describe people and places | adjectives agreement and position; c'est / il est | L1, R2 |
| 8 | Make and change an appointment | futur proche; dates; pouvoir, vouloir | L2, R2 |
| 9 | Fill in a form and write a short note | personal information; capital letters and punctuation | EE1, R1 |
| 10 | Talk about your home and your neighbourhood | il y a; prepositions; questions où, comment | EO1, L1 |
