# Exam item standard

1. Open `docs/exam-specs/TCF-Canada.md` and the section for the item type. Copy the format, timing, number of options and scoring facts into your work; don't rely on memory of the real exam.
2. State the CEFR band and the NCLC target before you write. Keep vocabulary and syntax inside that band.
3. Write original material. Never copy or paraphrase official papers or published prep books.
4. One defensible answer per closed item; write why each distractor is wrong in one line.
5. Writing and speaking prompts carry the task, the word or time limit and the rubric the grader uses.
6. Mark facts about the exam you could not confirm in the spec as `unverified` and stop for `exam-content-lead` to rule.
7. Hand the item to `exam-item-reviewer`; the author never reviews their own item.
