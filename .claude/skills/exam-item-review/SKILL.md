# Exam item review

1. Read only the item and `docs/exam-specs/TCF-Canada.md`; don't read the author's reasoning first.
2. Format, timing, option count and scoring match the spec for this section.
3. Answer the item yourself before looking at the key. A different answer, or two defensible ones, is blocking.
4. Band check: is the language what the claimed level would read or say? Name the word or structure that is above or below it.
5. Originality and claims: nothing copied; no use of exam trademarks beyond the permitted claims.
6. French is correct; instructions match the real exam's style.
7. No bias, no outside knowledge required.
8. Verdict first: `REVIEW: PASS` or `REVIEW: CHANGES`, then at most 10 lines of `label: item - problem - fix`.
