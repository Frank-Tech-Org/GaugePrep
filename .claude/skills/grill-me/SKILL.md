# Grill me

1. State what you already know from the repo and the ticket in two lines, so no question repeats it.
2. Ask one question at a time. Each question comes with your recommended answer and the cost of choosing wrong.
3. Prefer questions whose answer changes scope, order or a measurable number; skip anything you can decide yourself.
4. After each answer, say what changed in the requirement in one line.
5. Stop when the criteria can be written with numbers. Hand the result to `product-manager`; ask the owner nothing that `CLAUDE.md` "Who decides" gives to an agent.
